# Economics Society

The Economics Society of the Department of Economics at Pondicherry University is a student community for learning and discussing economics beyond the classroom.

We bring BA and MA students together to research, present, debate, and share ideas.

## What we do

- **Intra-Departmental Economics Colloquium (IDEC):** student presentations on current and research-focused economic themes.
- **Debates and discussions:** moderated conversations about contemporary economic questions.
- **Workshops and seminars:** practical research skills and perspectives from invited academics and experts.
- **Annual magazine:** student research, opinion pieces, and reviews, subject to editorial review.

The society is organised through five student verticals: Organizing, Logistics, Content, Finance, and Social Media.

## Membership

Membership is open to BA and MA students in the Department of Economics at Pondicherry University.

## Get involved

Students interested in participating or contributing can contact the Economics Society through the Department of Economics at Pondicherry University.
