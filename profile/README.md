# Economics Society

### Department of Economics · Pondicherry University

The **Economics Society** is a student-led academic initiative of the Department of Economics, Pondicherry University, created to provide a structured space for students to engage with economics beyond the classroom.

The Society brings together students across the **BA and MA programmes** through research presentations, discussions, debates, technical workshops, seminars and student publications.

Its work is centred on a simple principle: **economic education should involve not only learning theory, but also learning how to research, interpret evidence, present an argument and engage seriously with competing ideas.**

---

## About the Society

The Economics Society was conceived to strengthen co-curricular learning within the Department of Economics.

The Society provides students with opportunities to:

- research and present economic questions;
- discuss contemporary economic issues;
- develop public speaking and argumentation skills;
- work with economic and government data;
- develop practical research and analytical skills;
- engage with academics and researchers beyond the university; and
- publish student research and writing.

The Society is intended to serve as a peer-learning community across the Department, giving students a regular platform to learn from one another while working alongside faculty.

---

## Academic Activities

The Society's programme is organised around five formats.

### IDEC — Intra-Departmental Economics Colloquium

**IDEC** is the Society's principal research-presentation forum.

Students present research on a theme selected for each session, with participation from students and faculty of the Department. The format is intended to encourage students to move from reading economic ideas to researching, presenting and defending them.

Proposed areas include:

- **Current Indian Economic Issues**
  - Monetary Policy Committee reports
  - NITI Aayog reports
  - Economic Surveys
  - Union Budget
- **Global Macroeconomic Trends**
  - IMF World Economic Outlook
  - World Bank reports
  - Trade wars and tariff regimes
  - Global inflation and interest-rate cycles
- **Development Economics**
  - Poverty and inequality measurement
  - Human Development Index and alternatives
  - Sustainable Development Goals
  - Informal employment and the informal sector
- **Gender and Economics**
  - Female labour-force participation
  - Gender pay gaps
  - Unpaid care work
  - Women's financial inclusion and entrepreneurship
- **Social Justice and Human Rights**
  - Child labour and economic vulnerability
  - Bonded labour and modern slavery
  - Disability and inclusive economic policy
  - Economics of discrimination

### Debates & Discussions

The Society conducts structured discussions on contemporary economic questions.

Sessions may be moderated by a faculty member or student. Participants are encouraged to take positions they genuinely find persuasive and support them through reasoning and evidence.

Illustrative questions include:

- Should India adopt a Universal Basic Income?
- Is fiscal-deficit spending justified in an emerging economy?
- What should be the role of the state in a market economy?
- Is the gig economy exploitative or empowering?

The purpose is to create a serious environment in which students learn to articulate, question and defend economic arguments.

### Workshops & Seminars

Each semester includes a **technical workshop** and a **seminar**.

Workshops focus on practical skills relevant to economics coursework and research. Proposed areas include data analysis using Excel, Python and R; econometrics and statistical software such as STATA and EViews; literature reviews; academic writing; and working with RBI, MoSPI and NSSO data.

Seminars provide exposure to research and academic perspectives beyond the Department. Invited professors, researchers and other experts may discuss their research, fields of study and academic experience.

### Magazine

The Society plans to publish an **annual student-run magazine** featuring original work from the Department. It may include student research articles, opinion pieces, book and paper reviews, and other economics-related writing.

Submissions are expected to go through an open call followed by review and editing by the student board.

## Society Verticals

The Society's activities are supported by five functional verticals:

- **Organizing:** coordinates with faculty, arranges approvals, liaises with speakers and moderators, and oversees event schedules.
- **Logistics:** handles venues, seating, projectors, laptops, refreshments and other event arrangements.
- **Content:** develops themes for IDEC, debates, workshops and seminars, and curates material for the magazine.
- **Finance:** manages budgets, records expenditure and maintains financial records for Society activities.
- **Social Media:** communicates Society activities, encourages participation, maintains social-media channels and documents sessions.

## Membership

Membership is open to **BA and MA students of the Department of Economics, Pondicherry University**. Students may participate through presentations, discussions, workshops, seminars, magazine submissions, research projects and the Society's functional verticals.

Participation complements formal coursework.

---

## Working Principles

**Evidence over assertion.** Arguments should be supported by data, literature or clearly stated reasoning.

**Discussion over posturing.** Disagreement is expected; personal attacks are not.

**Research over repetition.** Students are encouraged to investigate questions rather than simply reproduce existing conclusions.

**Reproducibility where possible.** Document data, code and methods so others can understand and, where feasible, reproduce the work.

**Peer learning.** Students at different stages of study should be able to learn from one another.

## GitHub & Collaboration

The organization keeps Society work discoverable and able to continue across student cohorts. Use the repositories according to their audience:

- [Society Archive](https://github.com/Pondi-Uni-Economics-Society/Society-Archive) — approved public papers, presentations, event records and links to recordings.
- [Database](https://github.com/Pondi-Uni-Economics-Society/Database-) — shared, documented economic data resources.
- [Research Project Template](https://github.com/Pondi-Uni-Economics-Society/Research-Project-Template) — starter layout for a new team project; create Society projects under the organization and keep them private while work is in progress.
- Society Operations — private workspace for governance drafts, planning and handover; access is limited to its repository collaborators.

Project teams should record their question, sources, methods, decisions and handover notes. Publish outputs to the Archive only after author consent and appropriate Society review. Large recordings should be hosted on an approved platform, with only descriptive records and links in GitHub.

Do not put passwords, personal/confidential information, or restricted research data in GitHub—even in private repositories. Use university-approved secure storage for restricted material.
